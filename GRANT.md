# Implementation grant

This grant sits beside `LICENSE` rather than inside it. `LICENSE` contains the unmodified official
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International legal code, and adding sentences to it would create
a non-standard licence that no tool could recognise and no lawyer would enjoy reading.

**This file is the operative text. It is not repeated anywhere else, so there is no second copy to
drift out of step with it.**

---

## The grant

> Implementing this specification requires no permission from the Infinity OS Foundation. The
> licence in `LICENSE` governs copying and redistribution of the document. It places no restriction
> on software, services or systems built to the specification it describes.

---

## Why this exists

A licence on a document governs copying the document. It says nothing about building the thing the
document describes.

Most readers assume implementation is permitted, and most of the time they are right. But an
organisation with a cautious legal department cannot proceed on an assumption, and the question
reaches the specification's author over and over, which is friction on exactly the people whose
adoption matters most.

Saying it once, in writing, removes the question.

## What this grant does not do

It is not a patent licence and does not purport to be one. It is a statement that the Foundation
does not treat implementation of its published specification as requiring its permission.

It grants nothing on behalf of third parties. If a third party holds rights that read on something
in this specification, this document does not and cannot affect them.

## Scope

This grant applies to the specification in this repository. The Foundation's software, including
Infinity OS, is licensed separately under MIT, and that licence governs the software on its own
terms.
